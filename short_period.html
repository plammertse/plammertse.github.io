Short period

the short period mode

The short period mode is one of the two symmetric ( up / down ) modes of an aircraft. It is the stable wind vane mode controlled by the elevator.

  It is one of the two ( complex ) eigenvalues of the full, 4th order system matrix. The full equations can be simpli­fied in two ways: one way which brings out the key elements of the phugoid mode, and one which brings out those of the short period mode.
