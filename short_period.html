Short period

the short period mode

The short period mode is one of the two symmetric ( up / down ) eigen - modes of an aircraft. This mode is con­trol­led by the elevator.

  The eigen - frequency of the mode is one of the two ( complex ) eigen - values of the full, 4th order system matrix for the symmetrical motions. The "shape" of the mode ( the motion pattern ) is given by the accompanying eigen - vector.

  The full symmetrical equations can be simplified in two ways : one which brings out the key elements of the phugoid mode, and one which brings out those of the short period mode. This section will, among other things, describe the latter simplification.

animation

The animation gives a first impression of the mode's shape and of its frequency. The mode is often described as a pitch angle oscillation, but the actual motion is also, and in some ways primarily, a vertical bobbing up and down around the flight path.

  The motion is strongly related to that of a weathervane, or better, a dart arrow. It is also closely related to the oscillation of a dumbbell around its center of percussion.
