My name is Piet Lammertse, pronounced : Piet Lammertse.

  I was born in Amsterdam in 1951, and I have always lived in the Netherlands. I am an aircraft engineer by training ( MSc Delft Technical University, 1985, Aircraft Stability & Control ). I spent the best part of my life in R&D departments, first in aerospace and then in ( haptic ) robotics. Over the years I supervised some 100 university level graduation theses from interns. I am now retired, and I miss the work and the company.

  This website is my way to share what I think I learned, and would like to continue working on.

the website

This website touches on many subjects as they came up in the course of my work, or just because they piqued my interest. I am not in the business of writing textbooks ( with the near exception of the very elaborate section on propellers ); I am just cherry-picking items that I feel are normally glossed over or misrepresented in standard textbooks.



P. Lammertse, 2026
