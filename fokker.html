Fokker

Anthony Fokker was a colourful personality, and he was instrumental in spreading the gospel of the thick, cantilever wing. I plan to write here a few words about that glorious bit of history, and its decline and final downfall. In the meantime, you can read AR Weyl's charming book,

    AR Weyl,   "Fokker, the "Creative Years" .

Weyl stresses the role of Fokker's designers, in particular his long time chief engineer Reinhold Platz.

  His admiring successor ir. Beeling, Fokker's first academically trained employee in the 1930's, once told us : "There is a lot of terrible gossip in the book, but it sums up pretty well what it was like."

  By the way, these days I live one block away from Anthony Fokker's parental home ( and Lorentz's residential address, by the way ) in the Dutch city of Haarlem, but that is a total concidence.



P. Lammertse, 2026
