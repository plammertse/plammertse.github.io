propeller book

This site contains an original textbook on propellers.

  Intended as a simple introduction to the classical theory, it acquired a life of its own. It is now as solid an introduction into classical propeller theory as any, with even some small additions to the theory.

  in particular, it contains a simple but accurate analytical viscous optimization, and an introduction to vortex theory as an accurate alternative to Prandtl's brilliant, but approximate tip loss function.

  Comments are invited !
