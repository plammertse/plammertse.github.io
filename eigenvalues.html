the eigenvalue of a scalar system

Let us start by simplifying the matrix equation (1) on the state space page to its scalar version :

    x •   =   a . x + b . u (1)

If the constant "gain" a has a negative value, then this equation says that is x get smaller in time at a rate equi­valent to its own size, scaled by the factor a. The slope of the graph of x against time proportional to its height, with a factor. This is the graph of an exponential decay.

Physically x could be the velocity of a mass in oil. This is the equation of a viscous damper.
