the eigenvalue of a scalar system

Let us start by simplifying the matrix equation (1) on the state space page to its scalar version :

    x •   =   a . x + b . u (1)

If a has a negative value, then this equation says that x get smaller in time at a rate equivalent to its own size. The slope of the graph of x against time is proportional to its height. This is the graph of an exponential decay.

Physically, x could be the velocity of a mass in oil. This is the equation of a viscous damper.
