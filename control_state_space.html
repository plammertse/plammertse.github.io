SISO systems

The items under the Control top bar menu are primarily about single input, single output ( so-called SISO ) systems, typically the back-and-forth control around standstill of a single electric ( "servo" ) motor.

This branch of control is often called "motion control". Among other things, it occurs in haptics.

MIMO systems

Another interesting class of problems is that of multi-input, multi-output ( MIMO ) control systems, like the motions of an aircraft. These problems are discussed under the Aircraft header of the top bar menu.

In particular, they benefit from so-called "state space" analysis. This is discussed further in the Aircraft section.
