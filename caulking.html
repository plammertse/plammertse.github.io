Caulking

the new science of strong materials

In his lovely book "The new Science of Strong Materials", J.E. Gordon, discusses the endless chore of repairing the caulking in wooden ships.

Caulking is the process of applying a mixture of hemp rope and hot tar to fill the spaces between the planking of wooden hulls, to make them watertight.
